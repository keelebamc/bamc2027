Venue Information

BAMC 2027 will be hosted by the School of Computing and Mathematics at Keele University, located in Staffordshire in the heart of the UK. The conference will take place from 21–24 June 2027.

Conference activities will be centred in two neighbouring buildings on the Keele University campus:

  • Chancellors Building
  • Dorothy Hodgkin Building

Both venues provide modern lecture theatres, teaching rooms and informal spaces for networking and discussion, and are situated within a short walking distance of campus accommodation, catering facilities and parking.

Conference Facilities

Plenary lectures, mini-symposia, contributed talks and exhibition activities will be held across the Chancellors Building and Dorothy Hodgkin Building. Detailed room allocations will be published in the conference programme closer to the event.

Getting to Keele University

Keele University is situated approximately three miles west of Newcastle-under-Lyme and is easily accessible by road, rail and air.

  • Rail: Stoke-on-Trent railway station provides direct connections to London, Manchester, Birmingham and other major cities.
  • Road: The campus is located close to Junctions 15 and 16 of the M6 motorway.
  • Air: Manchester Airport and Birmingham Airport are both within convenient travelling distance of the campus.

Further travel information will be provided closer to the conference.
