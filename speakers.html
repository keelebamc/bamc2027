Plenary Speakers

BAMC 2027 is delighted to welcome five internationally recognised researchers whose work spans the breadth of contemporary applied mathematics.

Professor David Abrahams CBE

Isaac Newton Institute for Mathematical Sciences, University of Cambridge

David Abrahams is one of the United Kingdom's most distinguished applied mathematicians. His work has shaped modern understanding of wave phenomena and the mathematical techniques used to analyse complex physical systems. Throughout his career he has combined influential research with leadership across the mathematical sciences community, helping strengthen links between mathematics, engineering and industry.

Website | More Information

Professor Tiina Roose

University of Southampton

Tiina Roose works at the interface of mathematics, biology and environmental science. Her research combines mathematical modelling, computation and experiment to understand how plants and soils interact across multiple scales. Through extensive interdisciplinary collaboration, her work demonstrates how mathematics can contribute to global challenges in sustainable agriculture, environmental resilience and food security.
