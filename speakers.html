Plenary Speakers

BAMC 2027 is delighted to welcome five internationally recognised researchers whose work spans the breadth of contemporary applied mathematics.

Professor David Abrahams CBE

Isaac Newton Institute for Mathematical Sciences, University of Cambridge

David Abrahams is one of the United Kingdom's most distinguished applied mathematicians. His work has shaped modern understanding of wave phenomena and the mathematical techniques used to analyse complex physical systems. Throughout his career he has combined influential research with leadership across the mathematical sciences community, helping strengthen links between mathematics, engineering and industry. His contributions have been recognised through numerous honours and awards.

Website

Professor Tiina Roose

University of Southampton

Tiina Roose works at the interface of mathematics, biology and environmental science. Her research combines mathematical modelling, computation and experiment to understand how plants and soils interact across multiple scales. Through extensive interdisciplinary collaboration, her work demonstrates how mathematics can contribute to global challenges in sustainable agriculture, environmental resilience and food security.

Website

Professor Laurette Tuckerman

CNRS and ESPCI Paris

Laurette Tuckerman is internationally recognised for her contributions to fluid dynamics, nonlinear systems and scientific computing. Her research has provided fundamental insight into flow instabilities, pattern formation and turbulent behaviour, while her computational methods have influenced a wide range of disciplines across applied mathematics and physics.

Website

Professor Chris Breward

University of Oxford

Chris Breward's work lies at the intersection of mathematics, industry and the life sciences. He develops mathematical models that provide insight into practical challenges arising in manufacturing, biological systems and technological processes. His career has been marked by a strong commitment to industrially focused mathematics and collaborative research.

Website

Professor Anna Pandolfi

Politecnico di Milano

Anna Pandolfi is a leading researcher in computational mechanics and biomechanics. Her work focuses on the development of advanced mathematical and computational methods for understanding how materials and biological tissues deform, interact and fail under complex conditions. Her research has influenced both engineering and biomedical applications.
