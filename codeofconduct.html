Keele University Conference Code of Conduct

Purpose

The organisers of this conference are committed to providing a welcoming, professional, inclusive and harassment-free environment for all participants. We value diversity of perspectives, backgrounds, identities and experiences, and seek to foster an atmosphere that supports constructive academic exchange, collaboration and networking.

This Code of Conduct applies to all participants, including delegates, speakers, session chairs, exhibitors, sponsors, organisers, volunteers and guests, during all conference-related activities, whether held in person, online or as part of social events associated with the conference.

Expected Standards of Behaviour

All participants are expected to:

  • Treat others with courtesy, dignity and respect.
  • Engage professionally in discussion and debate, including where there are differences of opinion.
  • Be mindful of the diverse backgrounds, identities, cultures, beliefs and experiences of other participants.
  • Contribute positively to an environment that supports learning, collaboration and scientific exchange.
  • Follow reasonable instructions provided by conference organisers, venue staff and security personnel.
  • Respect the confidentiality and intellectual property of others, including unpublished research and personal information.

Unacceptable Behaviour

The following behaviours are not acceptable:

  • Harassment, bullying, intimidation or discrimination of any kind.
  • Offensive, abusive, threatening or aggressive language or behaviour.
  • Unwelcome verbal, written or physical conduct that creates an intimidating, hostile or uncomfortable environment.
  • Deliberate disruption of presentations, discussions, workshops, meetings or conference activities.
  • Inappropriate use of social media, photography, recording or dissemination of conference content without permission.
  • Any behaviour that compromises the safety, wellbeing, dignity or participation of others.
  • Any conduct that breaches applicable law or the policies of Keele University or the conference venue.

Academic Freedom and Respectful Debate

The conference supports the principles of academic freedom and open scholarly discussion. Participants are encouraged to challenge ideas, evidence and arguments through respectful and constructive debate.

Disagreement with a viewpoint does not justify personal attacks, intimidation, harassment or behaviour that prevents others from participating fully in conference activities.

Reporting Concerns

Participants who experience or witness behaviour that may constitute a breach of this Code of Conduct are encouraged to report it to the conference organisers as soon as possible.

Reports will be treated seriously, sensitively and, where possible, confidentially. Organisers will review concerns promptly and take appropriate action to ensure the safety and wellbeing of participants.

Consequences of Breaches

The conference organisers reserve the right to take action in response to behaviour that breaches this Code of Conduct. Actions may include:

  • Informal discussion and warning.
  • Removal from a session or conference activity.
  • Exclusion from part or all of the conference without refund.
  • Referral to relevant institutional authorities where appropriate.
  • Referral to law enforcement where required.

Commitment

By attending this conference, participants agree to uphold this Code of Conduct and to contribute to a professional, respectful and inclusive environment that supports excellent research, scholarship and collaboration.

This Code of Conduct reflects Keele University's commitment to dignity, respect, equality, inclusion, academic freedom and the creation of a safe and welcoming environment for all members of its community and visitors.
