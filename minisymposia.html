Mini-Symposia

BAMC 2027 invites proposals for mini-symposia showcasing exciting developments and areas of current strength within the applied mathematics community.

Mini-symposia provide an opportunity to bring together researchers working on a common theme and to highlight emerging research directions, established areas of excellence, and interdisciplinary applications of applied mathematics. Proposed topics may be broad in scope or focused on a specialised research area, but should be timely, scientifically engaging, and of interest to the wider BAMC audience.

Each mini-symposium will run for two hours and may include up to six talks of 20 minutes each (including questions). Organisers will be responsible for identifying, inviting, and securing speakers for their session and will act as session chairs during the meeting.

When preparing proposals, organisers are encouraged to consider diversity across institutions, gender, ethnicity, and career stage when assembling their speaker lineup.

Assessment Criteria

  • Scientific quality and timeliness
  • Relevance to the applied mathematics community
  • Breadth of interest
  • Diversity of speakers and institutions
  • Balance across BAMC themes
  • Evidence that invited speakers are likely to participate

The deadline for mini-symposium proposals is 22 January 2027. Successful proposals are expected to be announced during early to mid-February 2027.

Please note that BAMC does not provide financial support for mini-symposium speakers, and organisers should make this clear when extending invitations.
